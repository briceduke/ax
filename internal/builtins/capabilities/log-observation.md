---
id: log-observation
kind: capability
when: you measure something or learn a fact that a later chat should not rediscover
hints: [user-invocable]
---

Run `ax log observation "<finding>" --method "<how you know>"`. That save rewrites the root files. The finding is one fact. The method is how you got it: a command, a file, a bench. Do not log a guess. Do not ask for a compile step.
